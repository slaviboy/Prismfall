# Asset catalog

Third-party assets that Prismfall uses, with their source, licence and every change made to them. Original assets created for the project are listed in `ASSET_LICENSES.md` only.

## Photoscanned models (Poly Haven, CC0)

Real-world photoscans from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com). Every Poly Haven asset is released under CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license), so no attribution is required; the authors are credited here and in `ASSET_LICENSES.md` anyway.

The models are made real-time ready by `npm run assets:scans` (`scripts/process-external.ts`, see section 12 of `BLENDER_PIPELINE.md`):

1. `scripts/fetch-polyhaven.ts` downloads the 1k glTF, its `.bin` and textures into `assets/external/polyhaven/<id>/` and writes `source.json` (API URLs, authors, licence, polycount, download date, every file with its MD5).
2. `assets/blender/scripts/import_external.py` (Blender 5.2, headless) drops extra LODs, joins the meshes, collapse-decimates to the target in `assets/external/scans.json`, clears custom normals, puts the origin at the bottom centre, fixes alpha modes, wires ambient occlusion from the packed ARM texture, caps textures at 1024 px and exports `assets/blender/exports/scans/<id>.glb` with a contact sheet (`<id>.preview.png`) and a report (`<id>.report.json`).
3. glTF-Transform `optimize` applies the same settings as the Blender kits (meshopt, quantisation, WebP textures, no simplify, join or palette) and writes `content/shared/scans/<id>.glb` plus `<id>.meta.json`.

Orientation: Blender Z-up, exported Y-up; the side Poly Haven photographed faces three.js +Z (towards the default camera). The cliffs and the rock face are open shells scanned from the front, so their backs are hollow, exactly as in the source files.
